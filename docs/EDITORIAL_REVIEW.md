# Editorial review

Codex wrote and reviewed the updated English and Indonesian copy using the Website Maker homepage-copy guidance and the Zero Slop local editorial tools. No separate Zero Slop model or native-speaker panel reviewed it.

The rewrite covers service-group introductions and scope, nine mode summaries, fourteen bilingual operational dossiers, service-specific CTAs and FAQs, default FAQ answers, Home/About/Contact/RFQ narratives, route metadata and form guidance. Existing CMS-published articles and legal policies remain owner-managed. Their dates, commercial facts and legal wording were not invented.

## Checks and decisions

- Ran the repository’s existing source-only copy rules through `npm run test:copy`. An old Indonesian slogan in the fallback text registry was found and replaced. The guard retains its original forbidden-pattern assertions.
- Ran Zero Slop’s local `slopscore.py` on extracted source prose. Technical repetition remains because terms such as cargo, quotation, handling and chargeable weight identify specific concepts; synonyms were not rotated merely to lower a heuristic score.
- Performed a contextual read for unsupported company history, customer names, owned equipment/facilities, transit guarantees, invented credentials and numerical proof. None was introduced. Removed the baseline unsupported ten-year founder claim.
- Kept the distinction between customer submission and VORA validation, and between pre-payment documentation and confirmed cleared funds.
- Replaced literal Indonesian expressions such as “jendela waktu” with operational wording where appropriate. Trade terms, cut-off, handling, ready date, stuffing/stripping and similar industry terms remain where natural.

## Practical limits

The checks find wording defects, not authorship or factual truth. English readability heuristics do not certify Indonesian fluency. New explanatory logistics content was checked against the brief and existing service contracts; it was not treated as a line-for-line paraphrase with identical word or fact counts. Regulatory requirements still need commodity- and route-specific confirmation. No SEO ranking, conversion uplift, human-reader preference or disclosure claim was measured.
