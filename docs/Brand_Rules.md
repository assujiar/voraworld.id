# VORA — Extended Brand Rules

## Foundation
VORA makes international movement feel more accessible. Its promise is **Bringing the World Within Reach.** Brand attitude: direct, composed, dependable and globally conversant. Its voice is not inflated, jargon-heavy, or packed with generic logistics clichés.

## Logo
- The ligature VORA is always the master identity. Use the SVG, never retype it.
- Preserve the original negative space of O, the R-A transition, and the integrated red A counterform.
- The compact A icon derives from the A in the approved VORA wordmark; use in app icons, favicons, and small signifiers where VORA appears elsewhere on the page.
- Primary: ink/red on white or paper. Reverse: white/red on ink. Single-color: all ink or all white only for production constraints.
- Clear space: a minimum of half the visual height of the O. Any local standards that are more conservative override this.
- Suggested minimum: wordmark ≥120 CSS pixels or ≥25 mm in print; verify print tests for special material and viewing distance.
- Do not distort, outline, apply drop shadows, emboss inside digital assets, create independent triangles, add freight icons, or place the mark against busy footage.

## Color
Core: #101720, #B43E31, #FFFFFF, #F7F7F4. Neutrals: #64717C, #DCE0E1, #E9ECEE, #0B1219. Target visual mix: 70% white/paper, 25% ink/neutral, <=5% red. White on red passes WCAG AA for regular text at ~5.72:1; red on ink only ~3.15:1 and should not be used for normal text.

## Type
Digital: Inter Display 600 for headlines, Inter Regular/Medium 400/500 for body, Inter SemiBold 600 for actions. Editorial tracking in headlines -0.03 to -0.05em; body 0em. Body 16-18 px and line height 1.55-1.7. Headline line height 0.98-1.12. Avoid overusing all caps in long body copy; small uppercase labels use +0.12em tracking.

## Grid and space
Max desktop content width 1280px; desktop 12 cols, gap 24px; mobile 4 cols, gap 16px. Desktop page padding 64px where space permits and 20px on mobile. 4px base spacing; default section gaps 64-128px desktop, 48-72px mobile. Asymmetry and editorial whitespace are welcome; arbitrary overlapping glass effects are not.

## Components
Buttons min-height 48px; primary ink/white; secondary outline. Links underline with red; do not color entire anchor red on ink background. Cards 1px line border and 10px radius. Status indicators use labels and symbols in addition to color. Focus indicator 3px signature red offset 3px. Provide reduced-motion support; animation should be short and purposeful.

## Photography & visual assets
Prefer verified original photography of real facilities, teams and cargo processes. Crop around operational detail and scale with editorial restraint; no fake port maps, fake tracking screens, fantasy containers, random 3D glass trucks, artificial blue washes or generic smiling businesspeople.

## Social
Templates: feed 1080×1350 (priority), square 1080×1080, story 1080×1920. One headline + one supporting evidence block + branded footer. Guidelines for organic content: 40% expertise/proof; 30% solutions; 20% people/process; 10% campaigns. This ratio is a starting editorial guideline, not mandatory.

## Sales & printed material
Quotation: service/route prominently, validity, rate table, scope/exclusions, assumptions and a named contact where appropriate. Capability sheet: 1 promise, service groups, verified differentiators, contact. Presentations: one message per slide, a clear takeaway and a short source footnote for all performance figures. Do not include made-up metrics. Supply CMYK files following actual printing ICC profile and physical proof.

## Production checklist
[ ] Logo from approved SVG only
[ ] Accent is the approved A counterform, not an added element
[ ] Colors pass contrast and print requirements
[ ] Typography uses recommended sizes, line heights, and fallback
[ ] Content checked for truthfulness, dates and rate validity
[ ] Photos permission-cleared, not AI hallucinated
[ ] No layout overflow at 375/768/1440 px
[ ] Favicon and logo legible at export size
[ ] Link and button focus states visible
[ ] Deliver editable masters and export PNG/PDF/SVG as needed
